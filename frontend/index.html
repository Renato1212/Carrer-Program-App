In-Trade Checklist
  • Is price/volume behaving as pre-defined? If not, that IS the exit signal.
  • Scaling in? Ask: would I enter here fresh, with conviction?
  • Momentum dying near the target → take it; momentum strong → let it work.
  • Volume spike with no follow-through after an extended move = exhaustion.
  • Move the stop only on new information, never on hope.
  • The thesis had a clock — is the trade overdue?
