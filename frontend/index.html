🧭 In-Trade Checklist
  • Is price/volume behaving as I pre-defined? If not, that IS the exit signal (Day 4).
  • Scaling in? Ask: would I enter here fresh, with conviction? (Day 5)
  • Momentum dying near target zone → take it; momentum strong → let it work (Day 5).
  • Sudden volume spike + no follow-through after an extended move = exhaustion, cover (Day 4).
  • Don't move the stop except from analysis. No "free trade" fantasy (Day 5).
  • Time stop: trade thesis had a clock — is it overdue?
